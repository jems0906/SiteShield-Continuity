# Awareness Brief

## Accurate reporting

Record direct observations, timestamps with timezone, and the original source reference. Keep confirmed facts, hypotheses, and vendor assessments distinct. Do not collapse repeated distinct activations into duplicate ingestion records.

## Confidentiality

Use fictional or approved asset IDs and minimum necessary details. Do not put credentials, endpoints, sensitive floor plans, personal data, customer information, or unnecessary imagery in maintenance notes.

## Escalation

Use authorized employer channels when safety, continuity, or monitoring coverage is uncertain. Record who made a decision, the basis, when it was made, and who was notified. Follow employer emergency procedures in real situations.

## Handoff quality

List incidents, unresolved defects, work in progress, temporary arrangements, deadlines, and one next owner. Request and record explicit acknowledgement. Avoid implying that monitoring or a repair is verified when it is not.
