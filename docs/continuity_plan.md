# Continuity Plan

## Purpose and activation

Planning reference for a fictional storm-related site-access disruption and security-system vendor outage. The continuity lead assesses impact with site leadership and the vendor coordinator and records any activation decision. This plan is not a response runbook for real emergencies.

## Processes, roles, and recovery priorities

| Priority | Process | Accountable role | Dependencies | Approved alternative |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Employee safety and status communication | Site leadership | Verified site status, approved communications channel | Follow employer emergency instructions; do not travel unless authorized |
| 2 | Essential records access | Records process owner | Identity service, records platform | Manual queue log with later reconciliation |
| 3 | Payroll processing | Payroll process owner | Approved remote workspace, identity service | Approved remote work |
| 4 | Noncritical onsite work | Department owner | Normal site access | Reschedule |

For this fictional tabletop only, illustrative recovery objectives are payroll RTO 24 hours / RPO 1 business day and essential-records RTO 8 hours / RPO 4 hours. These are scenario assumptions, not approved or binding operational targets; actual objectives require organization-specific business-impact analysis and approval.

## Vendor coordination and decisions

The vendor coordinator requests a service-status confirmation and next update time through the approved contact channel. A named owner maintains each task, due time, and handoff. The continuity lead records decisions, rationale, timestamp, consultation, and escalation. Scope changes for maintenance require accountable-owner approval. No plan action disables a control or changes monitoring coverage.

## Communications

Employee draft: “A weather-related access disruption is under assessment. Follow approved continuity instructions; do not travel to the site unless authorized.”

Vendor draft: “Please confirm service availability and the next update time through the approved contact channel.”

Only authorized communicators publish external statements. Confirm facts before sharing and minimize personal or sensitive operational details.

## Training and testing

Orient participants to their assigned continuity roles before the exercise. Run a facilitated discussion-based tabletop at least annually in this fictional program, record attendance and decisions, and track after-action findings to named owners and due dates. Revisit the plan after material dependency or vendor changes. Do not treat a tabletop as live-response qualification.
