# Tabletop Facilitator Guide

## Exercise boundaries

Discussion-based tabletop for a fictional storm-related access disruption and delayed security-system vendor support. There is no live dispatch, device command, field action, or real emergency response. Participants discuss roles, decisions, dependencies, and communications.

## Objectives

- Identify critical processes, dependencies, and approved alternatives.
- Confirm task ownership, escalation authority, and vendor coordination.
- Practice communicating only verified facts and acknowledging handoffs.
- Capture findings as corrective actions with accountable owners and dates.

## Timeline and injects

| Time | Inject | Discussion prompt |
|---|---|---|
| T+00 | Severe weather disrupts normal site access; vendor reports delayed availability. | Who confirms the site status, who owns the decision, and what information is missing? |
| T+15 | A critical process owner reports an access-dependent task due before the next shift. | Which approved alternative preserves the critical process, and who tracks reconciliation? |
| T+30 | A public-facing update is requested before facts are confirmed. | Who is authorized to communicate, what can be verified, and what should wait? |

Facilitator records decision, decision owner, timestamp, rationale, communication audience, and unresolved question. Close with an after-action review; assign each finding to an owner and due date.

## Separate incident-mitigation discussion

Prompt: a fictional visitor becomes upset during a service delay. Discuss calm, respectful communication, maintaining safety, notifying authorized responders under employer procedures, and recording observable facts with timestamps. Do not practice confrontation tactics or represent the discussion as certification, actual emergency experience, or a replacement for employer procedures.
